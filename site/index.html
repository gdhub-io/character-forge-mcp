Character Forge MCP — a character studio inside your AI chat

A free, open-source MCP server for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and any chat that speaks MCP. Describe the character you need, give a picture, or ask for a surprise: three versions of the idea compete and the strongest becomes your character, with a card that answers nine questions about them.

Then, step by step and only when you ask: a concept sheet, their own place, an 8-second living picture, their voice in any language and a short theme of their own.
